Selected work

Exodus, 2022 to 2026

Representative examples of cross-functional problems brought to a fix over four years in Product Operations, where resolving an issue meant pulling customers, support, engineering, compliance and external providers into one loop.

Incident response

Limiting the damage from provider outages

Problem. Failing payment or swap providers leave customers retrying, spike support queues and blur recovery status.

What I did. Monitored live orders, switched failing fiat providers offline via remote config (typically within 30 to 40 minutes when no quick fix was likely) and disabled failing swap providers immediately on swap duty, coordinating engineering, support and partners through recovery.

Result. Cut repeated failed attempts during outages while aligning internal teams and providers on short-term mitigation and root-cause fixes.
AI workflows

AI-assisted support and product-ops workflows

Problem. Answering frontline questions on provider rules and app changes meant digging across Slack, GitHub and Notion or waiting on engineers.

What I built. Claude-powered workflows turning Slack triage into task lists and searching Slack, GitHub and Notion for context (including daily region and currency checks), plus a twice-weekly digest translating PRs across 4+ repos into plain-language user impact.

Result. Cut my own investigation time on engineering questions by an estimated 50%. Fed most of the weekly payments product-ops report to leadership, and shared the tooling with colleagues.
QA and launches

Weekly end-to-end purchase testing and regional launches

Problem. Payment-flow bugs and regional integration issues were reaching customers before anyone caught them internally.

What I did. Helped build and run weekly E2E testing: wrote scripts, briefed ~5 testers, triaged an estimated 20 to 25 results per cycle and coordinated fixes across engineering and external providers.

Result. Drove a payment-method and provider mapping fix, proposed a correction for a separate wallet discrepancy, and delivered data-backed rollout advice for 4 providers across Africa, Europe and Asia.
Product and SOPs

Reducing repeat inquiries through product and process fixes

Problem. Vague purchase-limit errors and undocumented compliance flags drove repeat customer inquiries and routine frontline escalations.

What I did. Collated recurring feedback, mapped manual verification, pushed engineering for clearer in-app limit messaging (plus proposed automated higher-limit grants) and helped write compliance-flag and order-error guides.

Result. Limit inquiries fell an estimated 50% week on week; escalations to specialists from frontline support dropped an estimated 80%+ over one month.
Tooling migration

Auditing a payment-support dashboard migration

Problem. Support was moving to a replacement dashboard without a side-by-side check of what frontline and risk investigations relied on.

What I did. Audited the outgoing tool against its replacement for product and engineering, then wrote an internal reference guide to its fields and investigation steps.

Result. Documented 9 gaps, 12 new capabilities and 9 recommendations pre-migration, including missing risk-score and KYC status fields needed to diagnose blocked users.
Provider reconciliation

Reconciling a 200-day order-status discrepancy

Problem. Failed provider orders still showed as "in progress" in the app, leaving customers confused for more than 200 days.

What I did. Investigated the status mismatch across internal records and provider data, aligned internal teams and followed through with the provider until reconciliation closed. Also pushed a provider to expose requoted sell-order statuses for engineering to display.

Result. The provider reconciled the stuck orders to display accurately, and engineering shipped clearer in-app status updates for requoted sell orders.

Editorial operations

Myhealthvoice editorial operating system

Problem. Tenbadgers' consumer health publication had a website and CMS, but no writer bench or repeatable workflow from topic approval through editorial and clinical review to sign-off.

What I built. Designed a 12-stage pipeline (stage owners, business-day targets, clinical risk tiers R1 to R3, written handoffs), built Notion article, people and hiring databases with self-checking formulas, exception views and an AI flagging agent, plus onboarding guides and CMS requirements for engineering.

Result. Built a 10-writer bench from zero in about 10 weeks (50+ screened), onboarded 13 contributors (10 writers, 1 editor, 2 medical reviewers) and commissioned 21 articles in 7 weeks, with 12 clearing final sign-off (first in 8 days). Handed daily management to an operations coordinator via a written routine.
